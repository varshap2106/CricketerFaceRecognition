End-to-End Pipeline

1

Face Localization (OpenCV Haar Cascade)

Detects frontal faces, verifies eye landmarks, and crops 160x160 aligned face regions.

2

2D Wavelet Frequency Decomposition (DWT db1)

Separates invariant facial structural frequencies (LL, LH, HL, HH) to eliminate lighting variation.

3

StandardScaler + PCA Dimensionality Reduction

Standardizes raw pixel + wavelet vector (16,384 dims) and compresses to orthogonal principal components.

4

Non-linear RBF Support Vector Machine

Calibrated probability estimation with tuned C and gamma hyperparameters for maximum precision.
